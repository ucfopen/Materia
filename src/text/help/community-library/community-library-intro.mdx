## Community Library

The [Community Library](/community-library) is a destination for instructors to share widgets 
they have created with the rest of the Materia community. 

Any instructor can freely upload widgets to the Community Library from their collection, 
or make copies of other instructor's widgets for use in their own courses. 
Once a widget is copied, you can edit it as much as you would like to tailor it to your students.

### Dashboard

The Dashboard is the main screen of the Community Library, and serves as an entry-point for discovering the perfect widget.
Here you can see currently featured widgets, as well as the top widgets in each of the four main categories: 
- **Arts & Humanities**
- **Business & Administration**
- **STEM**
- **Healthcare**

You can also click **see all** to view the rest of the widgets in that category.

Your institution may periodically select widgets to be **featured** at the top of the Community Library. 
A featured widget has been vetted by your Materia administrators to be high-quality and relevant, 
and will typically appear alongside other similar widgets. 

### Inspecting a Widget

To further inspect a widget from the Community Library, select its title card. 
This will open a new page with more detail about the widget, as well as various actions you can perform.

#### Preview Widget

This action allows you to preview a widget before you add it to your course. 
Pressing will open the widget in a new page, where you can play it exactly as it will present to your students. 

#### Copy to My Widgets

This action will make a copy of the widget, which will be avaliable in your My Widgets page.

#### Recommend

This action will add your recommendation to the widget. 
Recommendations signify that other instructors consider this widget *high-quality*, and as a result they will appear higher up in the Community Library.
You can recommend as many widgets as you'd like, but you can only recommend a widget *once*.

### Copying Widgets

Once you find a widget on the Community Library that you want to use, you can make your own personal **copy**.
This copy will exist independently of its library version, and you are free to use and modify it as you see fit.

To copy a widget, inspect it in the Community Library, then press the **Copy to My Widgets** button.
Once you copy the widget, it will appear in your My Widgets page, with a label indicating it came from the Community Library.

From here, it will be treated the same as any other Materia widget in your catalog, and can be integrated with your course just as easily.

#### Updating a Widget

The original author of your widget may choose to update the version that is avaliable in the library, changing its content.
Your widget will not automatically change if the original is updated, and will remain exactly the same as when you copied it.

However, if you wish to update your widget to the newest version, you can press the **Pull Latest from Library** button in your My Widgets page.
Doing so will replace the content of your copy with the new version, even if you have already edited it.

Updating your widget carries the same risks as editing a widget you have already published, and should not be done if the widget is actively being played.
 
### Sharing Widgets

To share a widget you have made to the Community Library, go to the 
[My Widgets](/my-widgets) page, and select the widget you wish to share.
There will now be a Community Library section, with a prompt to make your widget public.
Click the button next to this prompt labeled **Share to Library**.

After you click the share button, a dialog box will open prompting you to fill in information necessary for sharing your widget.

Widgets in the Community Library are required to provide a **discipline**, which represents the area of study your widget covers.
Optionally, you may also include the widget's **level of study**, or difficulty, as well as custom **tags** that you can add to make your widget easier to discover.

Once you have filled in the information to your liking, press **Share** to finish sharing your widget to the Community Library.
The page will now indicate that your widget is shared, and provides a link to find its entry in the library.

#### Removing a Widget

If you no longer want a widget you have shared to the Community Library to be avaliable, you can remove it at any time. 
To remove a widget, go to its entry in the **My Widgets** page, then press **Remove from Library**.

This does not revoke any existing copies of the widget from other instructors, but it does prevent new copies from being made.
If you change your mind, you can always share the widget again.

### Searching the Community Library

You can filter widgets in the Community Library by **discipline**, **course level**, 
and user-defined **tags**, as well as by the name of the widget itself.
These features make it easy for you to find exactly what widget you may need for your course.

Beginning a search hides the dashboard and shows the results of your search, along with some extra sorting options.
Your search results can be sorted by their upload date, the number of copies or recommendations, as well as alphabetically.

#### Tags

Tags are the most powerful method of search, and group widgets by a common theme.
For example, a widget covering the anatomy of a cell may include tags such as *#biology*, or *#cells*. 
However, the *#biology* tag may also point to widgets about natural selection or bacteria.

When searching the Community Library, you can include a tag in your search by typing the **pound/hashtag symbol (#)**.
This will open a dropdown underneath the search bar listing tags that best match your search, and the number of widgets that include them.
Adding more text after this symbol will further refine your tag search. 

Once you find a desired tag, click or press **Enter** to add it to your query. Your screen will now populate with all widgets that include this tag. 
Tags can be removed from your search by clicking on them again. There is no limit to the amount of tags you can search by.

## Common Questions & Issues

#### Can I re-share a widget from the Community Library that I modified?

No. Widgets that came from the Community Library, even if they were modified, cannot be republished unless you are the original author.

#### I found a widget that I don't think should be in the Community Library. What can I do?

Widgets that may be distruptive, offensive, spam, have incorrect content, or can be considered unwelcome in the Community Library can be reported.
You can report a widget by inspecting it, then pressing the "Report This Widget" button.
Reporting a widget notifies your Materia administrators, who will investigate further to determine if they should take action on that widget.
