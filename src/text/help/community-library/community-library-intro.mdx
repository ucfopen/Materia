## Community Library

The [Community Library](/community-library) is a destination for instructors to share widgets 
they have created with the rest of the Materia community. 

Any instructor can freely upload widgets to the Community Library from their collection, 
or make copies of other instructors' widgets for use in their own courses. 
Once a widget is copied, you can edit it as much as you would like to tailor it to your students.

### Browsing the Community Library

Use the library landing page to launch your search for the perfect widget. In addition to powerful search features,
the landing page highlights widgets across a variety of disciplines including **Arts & Humanities**, **Business & Administration**, **STEM**, 
and **Healthcare**.

Your institution may periodically select widgets to be **featured** at the top of the Community Library. 
A featured widget has been vetted by the Materia team and instructional designers to be high-quality and relevant.

#### Searching the Community Library

You can filter widgets in the Community Library by **discipline**, **course level**, user-defined **tags**, widget type, or simply by name. 
These features make it easy for you to find exactly what widget you may need for your course. Your search results can be sorted by their upload date, 
the number of copies or recommendations, as well as alphabetically.

#### Tags

Tags are are a powerful tool for fine-tuning your search. Use tags in conjunction with disciplines to locate widgets relevant to your 
course content. For example, a widget about the anatomy of a cell may include tags such as *#biology* or *#cells*. 

When searching the Community Library, you can include a tag in your search by typing the **pound/hashtag symbol (#)**.
This will open a dropdown underneath the search bar listing tags that best match your search, and the number of widgets that include them. Add additional 
tags or search terms for even more specificity. Tags can be removed from your search by clicking on them again or using the **backspace** in the search field. 
There is no limit to the amount of tags you can search by.

### Inspecting a Widget

To further inspect a widget from the Community Library, select its title card. The widget entry page contains more detail about the widget as well as various actions you can perform:

#### Preview Widget

This action allows you to preview a widget before you make a copy and add it to your course.

#### Copy to My Widgets

This action will make a copy of the widget, making it available in your My Widgets page. Once copied, you can modify the widget to suit your needs.

#### Recommend a Widget

This action will add your recommendation to the widget. Recommendations signify that you and others consider this widget high quality. 
You can recommend as many widgets as you'd like, but you can only recommend a widget *once*.

### Copying Widgets

Once you find a widget on the Community Library that you want to use, you can make your own personal **copy**.
This copy will exist independently of its library version, and you are free to use and modify it as you see fit.

To copy a widget, inspect it in the Community Library, then select the **Copy to My Widgets** option. Widgets sourced from or uploaded to
the Community Library will feature a **Library** label. Copied widgets are identical to your other widgets, and can be integrated into your course just as easily.

#### Updating a Widget

The original author of your widget may choose to update the version that is available in the library, potentially changing its content.
Your widget will not automatically sync with the original if it is updated, and will remain exactly the same as when you copied it.

You can choose to update your copy of the widget to the newest version by selecting **Pull Latest from Library** in My Widgets. However, be mindful: 
doing so will replace the content of your copy with the new version, potentially overwriting any edits you've made.

Updating your widget carries the same risks as editing a widget you have already published, and care should be taken if the widget is actively being played.
 
### Sharing Widgets to the Library

The Community Library is at is best when you and your fellow instructors share your expertise with the greater Materia community. You can share any of your published widgets
with just a few simple steps:

1. Visit [My Widgets](/my-widgets) and select the widget you wish to share.
2. Select the discipline and course level of the activity and provide any optional tags that are relevant to the widget's content. For example, a Crossword widget about Cretaceous-period 
dinosaurs may include tags such as **#dinosaurs** and **#cretaceous**. The tag input will suggest existing tags based on what you enter, or you can add a new tag to the system if it doesn't exist yet.
3. Select **Share** to complete the sharing process. Your widget is now available in the Community Library!

### Managing Shared Widgets

A shared copy of a widget in the Community Library is effectively a "snapshot" of the widget's content at the time it was shared. If the original widget is modified in the future, 
the copy uploaded to the library will not automatically sync these changes. Syncing can be performed manually by selecting **Update in Library**. Conversely, anyone who has created copies of the 
widget for their own use will not receive these updates until they choose to update their local copy.

#### Removing a Widget

If you no longer want a widget you have shared to the Community Library to be available, it can be removed at any time by selecting **Remove from Library**. Note that this does not remove or delete copies 
that have already been made by other instructors. If you change your mind in the future, the widget can always be shared again.

## Common Questions & Issues

#### Can I re-share a widget from the Community Library that I modified?

No. Widgets that came from the Community Library, even if they were modified, cannot be republished unless you are the original author.

#### I found a widget that I don't think should be in the Community Library. What can I do?

Widgets that may be distruptive, offensive, spam, have incorrect content, or can be considered unwelcome in the Community Library can be reported.
You can report a widget by inspecting it, then pressing the "Report This Widget" button.
Reporting a widget notifies your Materia administrators, who will investigate further to determine if they should take action on that widget.
